ABOUT / XIANGYU LU 陆祥宇

A curiosity that
crosses scales.

Postdoctoral Researcher · Zhejiang University

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Zhejiang University, based at the ZJU-Hangzhou Global Scientific and Technological Innovation Center. My research focuses on reliable visual perception under challenging imaging conditions, with applications in agricultural remote sensing and optical inspection.

I work on image restoration and super-resolution, annotation-efficient segmentation, and structure-aware visual analysis. My current research explores multi-focus defect perception and the transfer of visual models across optical components and imaging conditions. I also develop geometric verification methods and software interfaces that connect vision algorithms with practical inspection workflows.

I received my Ph.D. in Agricultural Electrification and Automation from Zhejiang University in 2025 and conducted visiting doctoral research at the University of Toronto during 2024–2025. I am the principal investigator of an awarded NSFC Young Scientists Fund (Category C) project on imaging degradation and early-stage weed perception, scheduled for 2027–2029.
