Xiangyu Lu 陆祥宇

Postdoctoral Researcher · Zhejiang University

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the ZJU-Hangzhou Global Scientific and Technological Innovation Center, Zhejiang University. My research focuses on computer vision for agricultural remote sensing and optical inspection.

I work on image super-resolution, weakly supervised segmentation, and geometry-guided visual localization. My current research studies multi-focus defect perception and few-shot anomaly detection in optical components.

I received my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University in 2025 and conducted visiting doctoral research at the University of Toronto during 2024–2025.
