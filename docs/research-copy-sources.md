# Technical Research summaries

The Research page uses technical subjects, mechanisms, findings, and applications in place of first-person activity or contribution statements. Published quantitative claims retain their dataset and experimental scope. Ongoing optical concepts are described as prototypes or evaluation targets.

- **EVADM / CropSR:** [publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0924271624003289). The altitude-dependent variance observation motivates VASA. The abstract reports SRFI gains of 27% and 6% at ×2 and ×4 on real-image benchmarks relative to its baselines.
- **PaddySeg / GBiNet:** [published paper](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/drones/drones-07-00083/article_deploy/drones-07-00083.pdf), Table 5. GBiNet_r2 test mIoU is 91.50%, versus 90.95% for BiSeNetV2, with lower FLOPs. The site does not generalize the hardware-dependent speed measurements.
- **Direct geo-location:** the same paper, conclusion and Appendix A, reports a mean deviation of 21 cm over 20 ground-control points, below 1% of relative flight height. It is listed as a companion method, not a separate publication.
- **GeT:** [author's model repository and abstract](https://github.com/luxiangyugo/GeT_model). The ImageNet-initialized model reports 98.14% accuracy and 1.16 million parameters on GLDP12k. The description retains this training and dataset scope.
- **Florence-2:** the homepage owner explicitly identified this model as the source of semantic guidance. The [official model card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Florence-2-large) documents prompt-based vision, region proposals, and grounding. The project description distinguishes semantic candidate generation from geometric refinement; it does not assert a particular model size, task token, fine-tuning recipe, or benchmark result.
- **Other historical projects:** the original public Research page and its linked materials, preserved in the date-stamped baseline clone. Summaries describe documented methods and outputs without introducing new numerical performance claims.
